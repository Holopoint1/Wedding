What to wear

A Touch of Tartan

Our dress code is A Touch of Tartan. From kilts and trews to a subtle detail in your hair or your shoes, we'd love you to add a little tartan wherever you can.

Tartan, big or small

Men are encouraged to wear kilts or trews if they would like to. No tartan of your own? A tie, sash, scarf or even a ribbon counts, and the tartan finder is a fun rabbit hole.

A few notes

  • We're planning an outdoor ceremony, weather permitting, and there'll be time outside through the day, so bring a layer to keep comfortable.
  • Planning on stiletto heels? Consider heel stoppers for walking on the grass.
  • Please avoid full white or ivory.
  • The bridesmaids will be wearing dark green.
  • Robertson Hunting Ancient is the groom's tartan, so please save that one for the groom.
