Questions

Frequently asked

If we've missed anything, please drop us a line.

Are children invited?

As much as we love your little ones, we have decided to keep our wedding day to immediate family children only (our nieces and nephews). We hope you understand and we can't wait to celebrate with you.

What is the dress code?

Our dress code is A Touch of Tartan. From kilts and trews to a subtle detail in your hair or your shoes, we'd love you to add a little tartan wherever you can. Men are encouraged to wear kilts or trews if they would like to.

What colour are the bridesmaid dresses?

The bridesmaid dresses will be dark green.

Is the wedding indoors or outdoors?

We are planning to have an outdoor ceremony, weather permitting (this is Scotland after all!). There will also be opportunities to spend time outside throughout the day, so we recommend bringing a layer to keep you comfortable. Also, if you are planning on wearing stiletto heels, please consider wearing heel stoppers for walking on the grass.

Can I leave my car overnight?

There are 25 parking spaces available. Guests can leave their cars overnight in the car park at their own risk but must collect them by noon the next day.

Can I bring a plus one?

Due to venue capacity, we kindly ask guests to attend only with the named guests on their invitation.

Do I need to bring a gift?

Your presence is the greatest gift. However, if you wish to contribute, we would love a contribution towards our honeymoon.

What time should I arrive?

Guests arrive from 1:00 pm. Please be seated a little before 1:30 pm so the ceremony can begin on time. Evening guests, please arrive for 6:00 pm.

How do I let you know about dietary needs?

Please add anything we should know to the dietary field on your RSVP. We'll be in touch closer to the day if we need any more detail.
